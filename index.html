Cincinnati, Ohio

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Cincinnati, working at the intersection of causal inference and health economics.

I spent over two years as a statistician and research data analyst at the Pennsylvania State University College of Medicine, where I analyzed neuroimaging and clinical data and co-authored peer-reviewed research on the neuroscience of olfaction, Parkinson's disease, and Alzheimer's disease. I hold an MS in Applied Economics (Econometrics and Quantitative Economics) from the University of Cincinnati and a BSc in Economics & Management, awarded with First Class Honours, from the University of London (London School of Economics academic direction). I am also a passed finalist of the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA).

Research

My research uses causal inference and applied econometric methods to study questions in health.
