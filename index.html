I am a PhD student in Economics at the Carl H. Lindner College of Business, University of Cincinnati. My interests lie in applied econometrics and its application to questions in health and human behavior.

Before beginning the PhD, I spent over two years as a statistician and research data analyst at the Pennsylvania State University College of Medicine, where I analyzed neuroimaging and clinical data and co-authored peer-reviewed research on the neuroscience of olfaction, Parkinson's disease, and Alzheimer's disease. I hold an MS in Applied Economics (Econometrics and Quantitative Economics) from the University of Cincinnati and a BSc in Economics & Management, awarded with First Class Honours, from the University of London (London School of Economics academic direction). I am also a passed finalist of the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA).

Research

My work brings econometric and statistical methods to applied problems. I am especially interested in the following areas (draft — refine to your own agenda):
